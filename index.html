Our promise

  • Offline first. Our apps do their work on your phone. If a feature ever truly needs the internet, we'll say so plainly and ask you first.
  • No accounts, no analytics of our own. We don't sell or scrape your data.
  • Honest about ads. Our apps are free because they show ads from Google. That is the only reason they use the internet, and Google's ads software collects some device data to show them. Each app's privacy policy lists exactly what.
  • Per app, in plain words. What an app does with your files is written in that app's own privacy policy.
