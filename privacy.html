Privacy Policy for Audalu

Honestware makes small Android apps. Audalu keeps your files on your phone, and we never sell your data.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

The short version

  • Your videos and audio never leave your phone. All conversion happens on your device. We never upload, see or store your files.
  • We don't have accounts, a server, or analytics. We (the developer) don't collect any personal data ourselves; only Google's ads software does (below).
  • The app shows ads from Google (AdMob). To show ads, Google's ads software collects some device data, described below. That's the only reason the app uses the internet.

1. Who we are

Audalu is made by Honestware ("we"), an independent app studio. Contact: honestware.apps@gmail.com.

2. Your files

If you allow it, Audalu can show the videos on your phone in its own gallery, so you can find them by name and folder. Android asks you first, and you can choose all videos, only some videos, or don't allow. You can still pick videos with Android's own picker or file browser at any time, and you can change this later in the app's Settings or in Android's settings. The app reads the videos you convert on your phone, extracts the sound, and saves the new audio file to your phone's Music folder. Your videos and audio are processed only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. The app never asks for access to your photos.

3. Data we collect

None by us. We don't run our own servers, accounts, analytics or crash-reporting tools. The only data collected is by Google's ads software, described in section 4. The app remembers a few things only on your phone: your preferred format, whether you've seen a tip, and which videos you've already converted. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK, which is built into the app, automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • App interactions with ads, such as app launches, taps and video-ad views;
  • Diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the ads software;
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. The data is encrypted in transit. How Google uses it: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your phone).
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Privacy options.

5. Notifications

If you allow it, the app shows a notification while a long conversion runs and when it finishes. These notifications are created on your phone and contain no ads.

6. Children

Audalu is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. For data collected by Google's ads software, see Google's policies above. You can also reset your advertising ID at any time.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions? Email honestware.apps@gmail.com.
